# Cheap Signal — Research Notes

**Purpose:** Evidence for photographer Instagram degradation / craft-community migration, LinkedIn endorsement cost-collapse (counterexample), plus Cosign thesis adjacency. Prefer primary reporting over vibes. Mark what we cannot claim.

**Researched:** 2026-09-25 PT · Critique pass additions same day

---

## Claim we *can* support

**Pattern:** Photographers who cared about still craft report that Instagram’s shift toward algorithmic feeds, Reels/video priority, ads, and engagement optimization displaced chronological photo community — and many diversified into Flickr, Glass, Substack, Discord, personal sites, or quit.

This is a **documented craft-community pattern**, not a market-share death certificate for Instagram.

**Causes are plural:** vanity/engagement metrics are one driver; **format shift** (Reels/video priority over stills), ad load, and spam are others. Do not overclaim “vanity-only.” Parallel: LinkedIn becoming influencer/AI spam while skill endorsements went to zero — multiple cheapening paths at once.

**Residue is a snapshot:** Flickr / Glass / Substack / Discord / personal sites are where craft attention is relocating *now*. Flickr already hollowed once before; Substack and Discord can follow the same arc if cost collapses there. Plural residue ≠ stable end state.

## LinkedIn endorsement decay (thesis-critical counterexample)

Public name-backed “belief” that **cost nothing and became reciprocal** went to approximately zero as a hiring signal. This is the gap that decides whether the Cosign thesis survives.

### L1. Sudarsan Santhanam — “The (F)utility of LinkedIn endorsements”

- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/futility-linkedin-endorsements-sudarsan-santhanam
- **What it establishes:** First-person account of endorsements from strangers for unobserved skills; reciprocal “scratch my back” requests; concludes rampant abuse emptied endorsements of worth; suggests forcing written justification or dropping the feature.

### L2. Forbes / Susan Adams (2012) — early endorsement mechanics

- **URL:** https://www.forbes.com/sites/susanadams/2012/12/04/everything-you-need-to-know-about-linkedin-endorsements/
- **What it establishes:** Contemporary how-to noting ease of tapping skills and social pressure to reciprocate — the cost-collapse design was present early.

### L3. McBrayer PLLC — professionalism / reciprocal dilemma

- **URL:** https://www.mcbrayerfirm.com/blogs-Employment-Law-Blog,do-linkedin-endorsements-create-a-chink-in-professionalism
- **What it establishes:** Endorsements are too easy; can come from people who have not seen the skill; reciprocal loops make them misleading; author rule: only endorse someone you would write a recommendation letter for.

### L4. ERE / Dr. John Sullivan — scarcity / stakes critique

- **URL:** https://www.ere.net/articles/linkedin-endorsements-good-idea-but-we-need-a-lot-more-than-it-offers
- **What it establishes:** Nothing at stake for endorsers; gamification weakened signal strength toward irrelevance; proposes artificial scarcity (e.g. endorse only one person as “best”) as a way to restore information value — directly adjacent to “keep cosigns costly.”

### L5. Recruiter consensus (2025) — hiring managers do not care

- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/therealestrecruiter_i-dont-know-who-needs-to-hear-this-but-activity-7343288226280820736-0f3t
- **What it establishes:** Practitioner claim that hiring managers do not use skill endorsement counts; comment thread echoes that endorsements became theater; some still value *written recommendations* more than taps.
- **Caveat:** Social post + thread, not a survey — use as industry voice, not as a measured rate.

**Synthesis for drop:** LinkedIn endorsements were the same *shape* as public attributed belief. They died when cost collapsed (one-tap, unlimited, reciprocal, no downside). Cosign only works if cosigns stay costly — specific why, reputation skin, or economic/IRL commitment — not “another endorsement graph.”

## Cosign adjacency (public thesis — for punchline accuracy)

### a16z.news — “How Silicon Valley Knows Its People” (Sep 25, 2026)

- **URL:** https://www.a16z.news/p/how-silicon-valley-knows-its-people
- **Core framing (paraphrase carefully):** Belief is private conviction you can invest (time, attention, access, reputation, money); affiliation makes belief legible; vouches (tweet, invite, intro, angel check) are forms of co-sign; as AI makes output abundant and attention scarce, the human belief layer matters more.
- **Use carefully:** A tweet-cosign and an angel check are both “co-signs” in that essay’s language — but they are **not equal cost**. Drop must separate cheap cosigns (tweet/repost) from expensive ones (hire, check, IRL invite).

### a16z Jobs Substack — Cosign introduction

- **URL:** https://a16zjobs.substack.com/p/introducing-cosign-a-new-space-to
- **Establishes:** Cosign as curated professional network for people/companies in the startup ecosystem; New Media product team.

### Public commentary on hiring flood

- Reporting around Moore/Elman Cosign discussions notes that AI-driven application volume makes undifferentiated outreach cheap and named endorsement relatively scarce. Treat specific “100,000 applications” style figures as **illustrative / attributed quotes if used**, not as drop-invented facts.

## What we **cannot** claim

| Tempting claim | Why not |
|---|---|
| “X% of photographers left Instagram” | No reliable census; reporting is qualitative + trade press. |
| “Instagram is dead” as market fact | Platform still large; craft users may leave while others stay. |
| Vanity metrics as the *only* cause of photographer exodus | Format shift (Reels), ads, spam are co-causes in the same sources. |
| Exact engagement-rate declines as ours | Attribute third-party estimates; do not invent. |
| Cosign internal metrics, roadmap, or private product details | Out of scope; do not invent; do not contact the team. |
| That Flickr/Glass/Substack “won” as a stable end state | Migration is plural and incomplete; venues can hollow again. |
| That making intros/reputation easier destroys Cosign | Thesis is **cost collapse**, not ease. Cosign *should* make costly belief more legible. |
| Fabricated closeness-widget brand or product | Explicit non-goal. |

## Research stance for UI copy

- Prefer “cost collapsed” over “platforms that make a signal easy destroy it.”
- Prefer “hollowed craft on the platform” over “platform died.”
- Prefer “many photographers report…” over “photographers left.”
- Prefer “what the feed pays” over “quality.”
- Prefer “expensive commitment vs cheap attestation” over “Cosign invented scarcity.”
- Residue venues = **snapshot**, not destiny.
- Every number either cited or marked **estimate**.
