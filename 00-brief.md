# Cheap Signal — Drop Brief

**Working title:** Cheap Signal  
**Seat:** Senior Product Engineer, a16z Cosign / New Media  
**Format:** MTS Drops–style interactive editorial briefing (not a Cosign clone, not an RFP app, not a landing page)  
**Rubric:** creative · decisive · quick · empathetic

---

## One-liner

Cheap signals fail when **cost collapses** — reciprocal, zero-stakes, unlimited. Cosign only works if cosigns stay **costly**: reputation skin in the game, not LinkedIn-endorsement theater. Scarcity is expensive commitment (hire, check, IRL invite), not “Cosign invented belief.”

## Emotional job

Make the reader *feel* the difference between a zero-cost attestation and a costly commitment — then leave them with a clear product intuition: Cosign survives only if the act stays expensive. Ease of reputation/intros/access is fine; collapse of cost is fatal.

## Section outline

1. **Hook** — Cost collapse, not “platforms that make a signal easy destroy it.”
2. **Mechanism** — Cheap attestation vs costly commitment; scarcity order by belief cost.
3. **Proof case** — Photographers as canary (vanity *and* format shift / Reels / ads); residue venues as a **snapshot**, not a stable end state.
4. **Interactive lab** — Regime control (Cheap feed / Mixed / Costly commitments) with defined **T**; re-ranking of “what the feed pays”; stacks that actually move with T.
5. **LinkedIn counterexample** — Why skill endorsements went to zero; what would keep a Cosign costly.
6. **Cosign punchline** — Cheap cosigns (tweet/repost) vs expensive ones (hire, check, seed, IRL invite).
7. **Sources** — Cited reporting; explicit non-claims.

## Interaction model

- Single continuous scroll with sticky section labels (drop energy, not dashboard chrome).
- Primary control: **T** — share of feed attention on zero-cost attestations (0 = costly commitments dominate; 1 = zero-cost attestations dominate), plus discrete regime presets.
- Re-ranks the same hand-labeled signals by **what the feed pays** (honest label — not “quality”).
- Cheap-attestation stack and costly-commitment stack **both scale with T** so dragging proves a difference.
- Headline readout: **costly share of what the feed pays** (moves hard across regimes) + current top-of-feed label.
- Secondary: expand a signal card for kind / cheapness / attributable belief / confidence.
- No sign-in, no forms, no CTA spam. Calm adult craft. Complete sentences.

## Scoring model — summary

Classify each signal by **kind**, **cheapness**, **attributable human belief**, and **confidence**; hand-label ideals; score cheap attestation against costly commitment. Full schema in `02-scoring.md`. Do **not** scan RFPs. Do **not** crown an Instagram like as “quality.”

## Non-goals

- Do not invent a brand name for any closeness / graph widget.
- Do not contact Cosign, Dhruv, or a16z New Media.
- Do not clone Cosign UI or pitch an RFP scanner.
- Do not fabricate stats; mark estimates clearly.
- Do not ship telegram-voice UI copy or growth-hack CTAs.
- Do not claim “Instagram is dead” as a market fact — claim the craft/format pressure pattern with sources.
- Do not imply Cosign fails if it makes intros easier — it fails if **cost** collapses.

## Kill tests

Kill or rewrite the drop if any of these fail:

1. **Could this be a Cosign landing page?** If yes → cut CTAs and product screenshots; keep the mechanism.
2. **Is the interactive a black box?** If the rank/stack cannot be explained by the schema cards → rewrite scoring visibility.
3. **Would a working photographer recognize themselves?** If the story feels like Tech Twitter cosplay → re-anchor in cited craft reporting.
4. **Does LinkedIn endorsement kill the thesis?** If unanswered → Cosign reads as LinkedIn-but-nicer. Address head-on.
5. **Does dragging T prove a difference?** If stacks and top-of-feed barely move → fix the model before shipping.
6. **Did we invent a number?** If a figure lacks a cite or “estimate” label → remove it.
